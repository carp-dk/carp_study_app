This app is used for health research studies in the Copenhagen Research Platform (CARP) at the Technical University of Denmark. If you have been invited to join one of our studies, this app helps to collect and visualize data from you. Depending on the study you join and after your explicit consent, collected data may include surveys (questionnaires) and health and activity data from Health Connect — such as steps, heart rate, exercise, blood pressure, blood glucose, oxygen saturation, body temperature and similar metrics.

By joining a study you can help researchers to better understand the connection between everyday activities and health at a large scale. Each study comes with a detailed description of its purpose, what data is collected, and who has access to the data.
